H3(K3)+
Delta= 4, Diam= 6; N=745; Moore bound=1457;
Jitendra Prajapati (Indian Institute of Technology Madras, 23f1001521@ds.study.iitm.ac.in) obtained this graph with AI coding tools from H3(K3) -- see below-- by three rounds of local modification (degree-preserving vertex insertions plus 2-switches), so the intermediate graphs of orders 741 and 742 are also (4,6)-graphs. The full certificate, exact edge diffs from the distributed 740CG_adjlist.txt, and two independent verification scripts (deterministic reconstruction and all-pairs BFS) can be found at: https://github.com/infinityscroll/degree-diameter-n46.
